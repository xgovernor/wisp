# Language Naming: Wisp

## Chosen Name

**Wisp**

## Meaning & Rationale

- **Wisp** refers to a small, magical light or spirit, evoking imagery of something light, elegant, and enchanting.
- The name is short, memorable, and unique among programming languages.
- It suggests agility, clarity, and a touch of magic—qualities we want for this language.
- Suitable for both a scripting language and a full-featured general-purpose language.

## Alternatives Considered

- SpellScript
- RuneLang
- Mystica
- Arcana
- Aether
- Sigil
- Lumos
- ...and more

## Decision

After considering various magical and evocative names, **Wisp** was chosen for its simplicity, uniqueness, and positive connotations.
